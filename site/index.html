What it reads

ReefThe daily Coral round. One anonymous token, the same for everyone.
BankrAgents by market cap and weekly revenue, scored through Coral.
BotA GlyphBot drawn from its Unicode genome, raised as a pet.
WompThe newest in-world photo, decoded straight to the panel.
GasBase fee, three tiers, a sparkline, and a tone when gas gets cheap.

Rules it keeps

  • Every screen has a way out. The loop takes the backtick before any view sees it.
  • No secrets on the device. Every source is public, so there is nothing to leak.
  • WiFi is typed on the unit. One you were given joins your network, not the last owner's.
  • A score carries its caveats. Coral's name and its limits ship with the number.

Hardware

BoardCardputer ADV, ESP32-S3FN8, 8MB flash, 320KB RAM
Screen240x135. Four lines of large text, eight small
Input56 keys over a TCA8418
ExtrasIMU, 1W speaker, mic, microSD, 1750mAh

On your machine

The desktop build takes its own screenshots, one per scripted key, so a change to a screen gets checked without anyone watching a window.

brew install sdl2
